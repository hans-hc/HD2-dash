# Data dictionary
All CSVs are UTF-8 with a header row. Dates are ISO calendar dates. Published_at_utc and retrieval timestamps are ISO timestamps with UTC offset. Counts are integers, averages and ratios are decimals, IDs are text, and Boolean CSV fields are True/False. Blank means unavailable or unanswered. Excel keeps identifiers as text, dates as sortable date values and fractions as numeric percentages. Acquisition time, source event time and report cutoff are distinct.

## Scorecard calculation contract
For anchor day D and an n-day baseline, count observations separately for D−n…D−1, D…D+3, and D+22…D+35 (inclusive). baseline_n, launch_n and later_n show actual counts. baseline_status, launch_status, later_status and activity_status give eligibility or a reason. pre14_avg, launch_window_max and day22_35_avg are calculated only when their own window is complete. A lift requires a complete positive baseline and the matching complete comparison window. An immature later window does not invalidate an independently complete short window.

- short_term_lift_pct = (launch_window_max − pre14_avg) / pre14_avg. Stored as fraction, displayed as percent.
- sustained_lift_pct = (day22_35_avg − pre14_avg) / pre14_avg. Same unit. The later mean describes aggregate peak concurrency.
- pretrend_pct compares the final seven pre-anchor days' mean with the first seven days' mean, relative to the first.
- prior_review_pct and event_review_pct are month-level fractions. review_change_pp = 100 × (event fraction − prior fraction) for comparable complete months with a common filter and positive volume. review_status gives eligibility.
- baseline_events, launch_events, intervening_events and later_events retain dates/names in their respective periods. confounder_count counts other documented events between the chosen baseline start and day +35. It indicates overlap, not verified causal interference.
- hypothesis_assessment and assessment_reason are blank without an owner hypothesis. A qualified threshold match with competing events is Confounded; a missing required input is Inconclusive. Supported/Not supported are descriptive judgments. No significance test is performed.

The Excel named tables use the sheet name without spaces plus Table. Players, Releases, Events and Reviews exactly mirror their CSVs. Decision brief is a scorecard projection. Event review, Hypotheses and Prioritization show keyed input copies. See [input handoff](inputs/README.md) for the explicit import workflow. The checked-in workbook is a dated review copy of authoritative Python calculations.

## Content-timing extension (November 5, 2026)
- `inputs/content_releases_2024.csv`: eight post-launch 2024 Premium Warbonds, keyed by content_id. release_date is the official stated date; announcement_date is the publication date. date_evidence is the exact date cue, with news_id, source_url and source_path locating the saved full post. Most dates are scheduled, not independently observed deployment times.
- `outputs/content_timing_scorecard.csv`: one row per Warbond. baseline/launch/later fields follow the existing score function (14 preceding days, maximum days 0–3, average days 22–35). Fractional lifts are ratios, not percentages multiplied by 100. week1/2/3 use complete seven-day means at offsets 0–6, 7–13 and 14–20. Missing windows remain blank with status/count fields.
- `cluster_anchor`: earliest official versioned patch within seven days before or on release_date, or the Warbond date if none. cluster_to_content_days is their calendar difference; preceding_patch_ids/dates list the included patches. cluster_* scores re-anchor both baseline and follow-up. Hotfixes count; the field does not imply equal patch scope, intended bundling or causal exposure.
- `outputs/content_timing_sensitivity.csv`: content_id × baseline_days (7 or 28), using the Warbond anchor. `outputs/content_timing_timeline.csv`: content_id × date, offsets −28 through +35, with daily peaks and known event names. Multiple case windows overlap and must not be summed as independent observations.
