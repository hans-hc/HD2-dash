# Owner handoff, in dependency order
1. **Player source supplied.** The original manual CSV is present in raw/. Keep a private copy outside Git. The daily portion covers all three events; the final 30 complete days have sampled maxima only. If complete daily peaks for that tail become available, preserve the replacement as a separate snapshot and follow inputs/README.md.
2. **Verify the chart and sources.** Confirm SteamDB's chart-day timezone and daily grouping. Inspect the selected official notes, Sony's dated amendment, the September 18 hotfix alias and the wider wiki date discrepancies. Save the evidence for any correction against its stable ID.
3. **Record retrospective hypotheses.** If possible do this before viewing charts. If results have already been seen, say so. Enter actual date, direction, metric and threshold in inputs/hypotheses.csv. Leave unanswered assessments blank.
4. **Review classifications and results.** Open curated/hd2_data.xlsx. Verify the mixed patch labels, near-date Warbonds, window counts and partial months against the source register. Use inputs/README.md for Excel-to-CSV edits. Rebuild and revalidate. The case study and Markdown findings are dated editorial snapshots; revise them if the numbers change.
5. **Prioritize.** Compare the three options. Record actual owner ratings, final ranking, confidence and rationale in inputs/decision_inputs.csv. Confirm any effort assumptions with appropriate team knowledge before interpreting them as estimates.
6. **Optional research.** Use the separate player and analyst guides if appropriate. Record only actual feedback and any resulting change. There are no completed interviews in this project.
7. **Approve or revise the proposal.** Decide the primary metric, cohort definition, thresholds, guardrails and what would reverse the choice. Mark the date and approver in the decision log. Revise preliminary judgments as needed.
8. **Set up Jira.** Review the 16 local issues and follow jira/jira_setup.md to map/import, link dependencies and verify statuses yourself. Track issued keys separately.
9. **Assemble Power BI on Windows.** Follow powerbi/build_guide.md. Verify both pages, record a refresh and save a PBIP if supported. The M/DAX kit and native report are distinct deliverables.
10. **Publish selected Confluence pages.** Apply confluence/index.md's manual guide, update relative links and permissions, and retain approval status.
11. **Finalize the case study.** Explain the decisions, reviews and lessons from the analysis. Check that every accomplishment is supported by completed work and incorporate actual feedback.
12. **Record the walkthrough.** Open with the September versus June comparison, show the May caveat, trace one number to source and close with the decision criterion.
13. **Publish when ready.** Select approved documentation and charts, verify private exports remain excluded, then publish the portfolio and repository using your own accounts.
