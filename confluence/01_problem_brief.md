# Which update deserves a follow-up?
A LiveOps producer needs to decide whether the available evidence justifies changing patch and Warbond timing. Hansen selected content timing as the next investigation on November 5, 2026. The original three cases establish the difference between a launch spike and later activity; an exploratory extension compares eight post-launch 2024 Warbonds. The immediate deliverable is a release-sequence review and a recommendation grounded in public data. A schedule change would need stronger evidence and production constraints.

## Reader and decision criteria
The intended reader knows live service production and needs the size, duration and ambiguity of each signal. A launch-week spike can reflect curiosity, a weekend, or people spending longer in existing sessions. A producer choosing between two features needs to know whether the change persisted and which competing explanations remain unresolved.

## Three deliberate contrasts
June combines loadout changes, stability work and a same-day Warbond. September revisits weapon viability with armor and enemy changes. May concerns access and trust, including an announced account requirement and its reversal. The cases were selected for contrast before comparing results. They cannot establish a general ranking of update types.

## Evidence boundary
Steam daily peaks describe simultaneous play on one platform. Monthly Steam reviews describe self-selected contributors. Official notes establish the timing and stated intent of a change. Missing pieces include who returned, why they returned, console behavior, purchases and the team's effort. A retrospective hypothesis may be recorded before reading the charts, with the true recording date. The owner's wording and thresholds stay open until supplied.

## Delivery and success
Deliver a traceable scorecard, review workbook, source register, two-page Power BI build kit, project backlog and one proposal with a measurement plan. Success means a reviewer can reproduce a number from a saved source, identify a credible competing explanation, compare the three options and state what evidence would change the recommendation. The live dashboard, interviews, publishing, Jira import and final approval are separate human activities.
