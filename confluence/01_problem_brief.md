# Project overview
This independent Helldivers 2 case study asks whether spacing patches and Warbonds was associated with sustained Steam player activity. A Warbond is a pack of unlockable equipment and cosmetics.

I began with three event studies, then compared eight post-launch 2024 Warbonds. I used a manually exported SteamDB CSV and saved official Steam announcements, calculated the comparisons in Python, prepared an Excel review workbook, and built a two-page report in Power BI web on my Mac.

## Main finding
Similar release gaps had different outcomes. Four of five releases with a two-day gap had later activity below baseline. The two same-day examples also differed. I couldn't recommend one gap over another from this sample.

## Recommendation
Make a 35-day release review part of LiveOps planning. Check the launch response, weekly activity and the later average alongside what shipped and other nearby updates. Compare similar content before recommending a standard release gap.

## Limits
This is a retrospective comparison of public Steam data. It does not identify individual retention, sales or console behavior, and it does not isolate timing's effect. The proposed release-review process has not been implemented by the game team.
