# Helldivers 2 - LiveOps case study
**Author: Hansen Cheng.** Independent public-data analysis of eight 2024 Warbond releases.

## Question and conclusion
Was staggering patches and Warbonds associated with sustained Steam activity? Similar release gaps had different results. I recommend a 35-day review of releases, considering content scope and nearby updates, before choosing a standard gap.
